our story

A Sanctuary of Beauty & Calm

Celeste Beauty Lounge began in 2015 with a simple dream — to create a space where every woman feels celebrated. From our plush leather styling chairs and botanical ceilings to the lavender tea we serve at your arrival, every detail is designed for your comfort.

Our certified stylists and aestheticians bring over a decade of artistry in hair, skin and nail care — blending premium products, gentle techniques and a touch of magic to every appointment.

  • Certified & award-winning beauty artists
  • Premium, cruelty-free products only
  • Hygiene-first, spa-serene private suites
